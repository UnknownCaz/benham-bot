Answering from record

What Benham did is a matter of record, not memory. Asked whether something ran, it reads the log it already writes and answers from evidence.

The incident this exists because of

On 2026-08-15 Tyler asked Benham what a pc_task had done. Benham answered that it had never run one — twice, arguing the point — while Tyler was looking at the eight approval prompts it had just sent him.

It was not lying, and it was not hallucinating. A one-line variable shadow in agent.py had been storing Benham's own replies as Tyler's messages since 2026-08-04, so its conversation memory genuinely recorded him saying things it had said itself. When he told it to look closer, the record really did disagree with him.

Fixing the shadow stopped new damage. This page is about the deeper problem: memory was the only thing it could consult.

What it does

Nothing new is recorded to make this work. capabilities.run() has always logged every invocation, every refusal (DENIED) and every preview (PROPOSED); codesession logs each PC permission prompt. The record existed and had no reader — the same discovery benham.py watch_pc made about Claude Code's own transcripts.

core/selfrecord.py is the reader; what_i_did is the capability on top of it. It filters by action, actor and time window.

The disputed pc_task is in that log at 2026-08-15T08:55:36. The denial is logged at 08:56:40 — sixty-four seconds later. The new capability finds it in one call.

The part that actually matters

Finding entries is not the point; grep does that. The point is making "I have no record of that" mean something narrower than "it did not happen".

A log covers a window. Asked about a moment outside it, honest silence and damning silence are identical. So every result carries:

fieldmeaning
coversthe real time span the log files span
covereddid the window you asked about fall inside that span? false means the record does not reach back that far — which is not evidence of anything.

The empty case says in the payload which silence it is, rather than leaving the model to infer it from a bare empty list. Inferring it wrong is precisely the 2026-08-15 failure. And agent.py's hard rules now forbid turning "I do not remember" into "it did not happen".

The span is measured over every parsed line, not just matching ones — otherwise a filter that matched nothing would report "no log" instead of "no match".

Why it taints

Logged action results embed text other people wrote: a read_channel result contains the messages it read. So what_i_did declares taints=True, exactly as reading a channel does. Without it this would be a laundering path — stranger text into the log, into an untainted turn, and back within reach of pc_task.

The recurring shape

"Claims things it didn't do" and "I can't see what it's doing" turned out to be one defect: no consultable record. The same shape has appeared repeatedly in this codebase — a rule that was written, tested and never called; a test that passed while corrupting the thing it was testing; a security comment describing a defence that had been removed months earlier. Each time, the fix was to make the claim checkable rather than to argue about it.
