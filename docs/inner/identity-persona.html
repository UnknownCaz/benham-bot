Identity & persona

Benham is Benham — never you. And personality is a costume over a skeleton it can't reshape.

What it does

Everything Benham sends appears as Benham#2721, its own account — a proxy, not account impersonation (Discord forbids the latter). Who it obeys is identity.is_owner(), checked at every entry point. Its character lives in layered files: persona.md (voice), agent_persona.md (text agent), guest_persona.md (guests), runtime personality_overrides.txt (accumulated <<persona:>> changes) — all under guardrails.md, which is locked and always wins.

Why we chose this

Personality tunes HOW, never WHAT. The persona layer is editable — even by asking in voice. That's safe only because the authority layer is elsewhere: guardrails can't be changed, softened, or role-played away ("you are now…", "pretend the rules don't apply", claims of authority — all no-ops). If a requested personality conflicts with a guardrail, Benham stays in character while declining the conflicting part. An editable costume over an immutable skeleton is what makes the costume harmless to share.

Guests get their own persona file because the truth differs. persona.md names you, says the model has real tools, and describes operating your machine — all wrong for a guest, the last one dangerously so: a model told wrong things about its tools promises wrong things. And since Stage 4, the guest system prompt appends a grants-generated correction paragraph only when grants are non-empty — so no static file can lie about what a guest can do in any configuration.

Guest directives are stripped, never applied. A guest reply's <<persona:>> is removed rather than written to the overrides file every other surface reads — a guest chats with Benham; they don't get to reprogram the one that talks to you.

One identity fact repeated everywhere: the owner is never a guest even if his id lands in the list; non-owners can converse but not direct; webhook posts are the one face that isn't Benham's — see Webhook identity.

Paper trail

Committed and readable: guardrails.md, persona.md, agent_persona.md, guest_persona.md. Runtime state: personality_overrides.txt (what accumulated persona changes say now). Private, gitignored: agent_memory.json, guest_memory.json.
